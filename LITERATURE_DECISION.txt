NEW CLOSEST-OVERLAP CHECKS

1. ProSocialTuning: Towards Understanding Task-agnostic Debiasing Through the
Lenses of Intrinsic Bias and Forgetfulness (Findings of ACL 2024).
https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.109.pdf
Read abstract, Section 3/Figure 1, method excerpts in Section 5 and evaluation
excerpts in Section 6. The study explicitly investigates bias relearning after
debiasing, varying downstream gender-related data proportions and sample counts.
Its method regularizes debiased attention heads; evaluation includes BERT and
RoBERTa, StereoSet and downstream tasks. This defeats a novelty claim based on
whether bias returns or whether downstream data composition matters. The inspected
measurements do not establish the exact centered occupation-profile trajectory
decomposition proposed here. That limited observation is not proof of absence.

2. Chained Tuning Leads to Biased Forgetting.
https://arxiv.org/html/2412.16469
Read introduction, task definitions, Section 3 evaluation equations 1-2 and
results/conclusion excerpts. It measures both overall and demographic-group
forgetting during chained tuning, including their difference and worst-group
behavior. Thus 'previous recovery work only reports aggregate scores' is false.
Our candidate distinction is an original occupation-score direction and its
amplitude, not simply group-disaggregated evaluation. Whether that distinction
adds enough scientific value still needs evidence. Future references must pin
the retrieved version; this unversioned source is a discovery reference only.

3. Co2PT (Findings of EMNLP 2023).
https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.390/
Read primary bibliographic record and abstract only. It proposes counterfactual
contrastive prompt tuning and tests extrinsic bias across downstream benchmarks,
including adaptation to already debiased models. This supports the relevance of
downstream debiasing comparisons. No claim about its full recovery metrics is
made without reading the paper's methods.

ALLOWED WORKING CONTRIBUTION
An exploratory comparison of test-set mean preference, strength along the original
centered occupation pattern, and residual profile changes, measured during
continued training relative to an equally trained uncorrected reference.
Strength/similarity decomposition is elementary mathematics, not a new algorithm.
Value would come from a reproducible empirical finding and evidence that this
decomposition materially changes an interpretation based on aggregate scores.

DISALLOWED CLAIMS
First demonstration of returning bias; first disaggregated forgetting measure;
proof of internal imprint/erasure; lexically filtered text has no relevant signal;
small-model pronoun parity establishes social fairness; three seeds or two models
automatically meet a journal/conference standard.
